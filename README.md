# Group-12-s-Final-Research-Project
Title: The Relationship Between CBE Birr Usage and  Financial Inclusion Among Customers in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

1. Project Description
This research examines the relationship between CBE Birr usage and financial inclusion among customers in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

- report/ – Final research report.
- presentation/ – Research presentation slides.
- meeting-notes/ – Group meeting records and decisions.
- references/ – Research sources and citations.
